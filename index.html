Our mission

Teach disability awareness before high school, and give teachers the tools to follow through.

We aim to foster disability awareness by teaching students about common disabilities and empowering educators to support inclusive classrooms. The focus is Ridgewood High School in New Jersey, and the students heading there.

Awareness is not pity. It is understanding someone’s challenges, knowing how to support them, and refusing ableist shortcuts like calling a struggling student lazy.

Why this matters

Middle school is the moment to get this right.

In-person activities help students understand people with disabilities, not to feel sorry for them, but to know how to show up as classmates. Early education cuts down unintentional discrimination before high school.

Teachers set the tone for the whole room. When they have the materials and language for learning accommodations, the class becomes a place disabled students can actually belong.
