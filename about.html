About us

Six cofounders, one mission.

BridgED began as a student effort in Ridgewood, New Jersey: teach disability awareness, support educators, and make school a place where disabled students are understood.

The team

Built by students who wanted classmates to actually know this.

The cofounders started BridgED because they saw the gap between disabled students and their peers: misunderstanding, too little exposure, and classrooms that were not set up to support learning accommodations.

Cofounders

The six of us

Kathryn Shin

President

Kathryn cofounded BridgED after receiving learning accommodations herself and watching family and friends move through school with disabilities. She wanted students and teachers to know the disabilities that show up in classrooms, and the laws that protect people who have them. She placed first at National History Day for research on the Americans with Disabilities Act, then presented that work at the Smithsonian National Museum of American History in Washington, D.C.

Devyn Letscher

Event Coordinator

Devyn feels it is important that her peers are educated on disabilities that many people have, such as ADHD, to create a more inclusive environment. By educating those around her, students with disabilities can feel less overlooked and more involved at school. She loves to teach others and hopes to make a greater impact by informing students of disabilities and the effects they have on people through planning interactive stations.

Josh Jung

Event Coordinator

Having a cousin who is blind, Josh has seen firsthand some of the challenges individuals with disabilities can face and has been motivated to promote greater awareness, understanding, and inclusion. He interned for New Jersey State Senator Kristin Corrado, a member of the Legislative Disability Caucus. Through BridgED, he hopes to help create meaningful connections between disabled and non-disabled students while raising awareness about disability rights and inclusion.

Cherie David
