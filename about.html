About us

Six cofounders. Kathryn stayed to keep building it.

BridgED began as a student effort in Ridgewood, New Jersey: teach disability awareness, support educators, and make school a place where disabled students are understood. The team is six cofounders. Kathryn Shin is the original cofounder who stayed.

The founding team

A group of six, built around one through-line.

The cofounders started BridgED because they saw the gap between disabled students and their peers — misunderstanding, too little exposure, and classrooms that were not set up to support learning accommodations.

Drop names and portraits into the five open cards below as you have them. Kathryn’s card is filled in now.

Cofounder who stayed

Kathryn Shin

Kathryn co-founded BridgED after receiving learning accommodations herself and watching family and friends move through school with disabilities. She wanted students and teachers to actually know the disabilities that show up in classrooms — and the laws that protect people who have them.

She placed first at National History Day for research on the Americans with Disabilities Act, then presented that work at the Smithsonian National Museum of American History in Washington, D.C. She now leads BridgED’s work with students and educators.
