Disability Rights & Education Bills

504 Plan

A civil rights amendment included in the Rehabilitation Act. It prohibits discrimination based on disability in programs or activities that receive federal financial assistance. Examples:

  • Extended test time
  • Preferential seating

Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)

Ensures equal opportunity for individuals with disabilities in schools and public/private spaces. Examples:

  • Ramps and Braille panels
  • Equal access to extracurriculars

Individualized Education Program (IEP)

A legal document for eligible students. Includes:

  • Instruction/support from special ed teachers
  • Accommodations like breaks or extended test time

Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA)

Guarantees children with disabilities free, appropriate education in the least restrictive environment. Provides funding and related services.
